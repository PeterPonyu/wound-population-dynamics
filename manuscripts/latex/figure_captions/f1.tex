\paperfigure{figure1_workflow}{fig:p2f1}{Information available in the two temporal prediction tasks. Three healthy human donors contribute skin and wound specimens on days 0, 1, 7 and 30. (A) Global timepoint holdout excludes every day-7 cell from model fitting and scaling. Blue tiles identify training samples; teal outlines also identify the observed day-1 source of prediction; hatched orange tiles are evaluation targets. (B) One illustrative donor-transfer fold excludes all cells of the third donor from fitting and scaling. Only its day-1 cells initialize prediction, its day-7 cells are evaluated, and its grey day-0/day-30 samples are unused. All four times remain available in the other two donors, and the test-donor role is rotated across all three. (C) A frozen expression map and training-only scaling precede prediction and energy-distance comparison with an unchanged source distribution. Time coordinates, donor count, training seeds, integration resolution and evaluation sampling provide complementary sensitivity checks. The arrays describe samples, not tracked cells; neither task observes diabetic-foot-ulcer healing or time to closure.}
