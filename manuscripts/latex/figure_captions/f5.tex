The line joins seed-0 mean held-out energy distances, 0.5496 at k=1 and 0.3854 at k=2; symbols identify seeds 0, 1 and 2. At k=1, the two single-training-donor choices are averaged within each held donor before equally weighting the three held donors. (B) Points are seed-specific reductions in mean energy distance (ED), k=1 minus k=2. The horizontal line is the three-seed mean, 0.165. Shading is a percentile 95\% interval [0.081, 0.315] from 4,000 resamples of three held-donor blocks after seed averaging; it is not an interval for each seed point. Evaluation subsampling is fixed across seeds. The largest configuration-specific range is 0.368. Overlapping training sets limit interval interpretation, and two k values cannot identify a plateau or required cohort size.}
