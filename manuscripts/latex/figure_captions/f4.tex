\paperfigure{figure4_methods_benchmark}{fig:p2f4}{Method comparison across three held-out donors. (A) Bars are arithmetic mean energy distances for stay-still and five predictors evaluated on the same donor folds. CFM means conditional flow matching; OT is the entropic optimal-transport displacement extension. Shared CFM has the lowest point estimate (0.395). Each predictive method improves over stay-still by more than the single split-half target reference in 3/3 folds; this is a descriptive criterion, not a significance test or population method ranking. (B) Each line connects shared and donor-conditioned errors for one of six training-donor comparisons (two retained donors in each of three fits). Conditioning improves all six. These training-donor fits share donors and do not measure transfer to an unseen donor. Neither panel shows uncertainty intervals.}
