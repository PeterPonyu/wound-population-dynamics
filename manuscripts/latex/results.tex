\subsection{A held-out acute human time point can be reconstructed under the fixed protocol}

Global timepoint holdout removes day 7 from every donor, whereas donor transfer removes one donor and retains all four times in the others. Both use the frozen representation and the training-only scaling in Equation~\eqref{eq:p2scale}. The product bridge in Equation~\eqref{eq:p2bridge}, field objective in Equation~\eqref{eq:p2cfm} and initial-value prediction in Equation~\eqref{eq:p2ode} specify a reproducible estimator, not an observed cell transition. This distinction determines whether the target time itself has been observed during training; the permitted source, target exclusions and fitting settings are recorded in Table~\ref{tab:p2protocols}.

A fixed-seed refit assessed numerical resolution of the RK4 update in Equation~\eqref{eq:p2rk4} and sensitivity to evaluation sampling. Across 25, 50, 100 and 200 RK4 steps, the target energy distance remained 0.705395 and the improvement remained 49.863\%; the largest discrepancy from the 200-step coordinates was below $6\times10^{-7}$ in root-mean-square units (Table~\ref{tab:p2numerics}). Repeated evaluation at 250, 500 and 1,000 cells per distribution gave median improvements of 48.46\%, 48.39\% and 49.26\%, respectively. Gain exceeded the same-count split-half reference in all 50 draws at each count (Table~\ref{tab:p2sampling}). These results assess one fitted field and the observed cells; the ranges across draws quantify evaluation sensitivity, not additional donor replication or confidence in clinical generalization.

Using the empirical weighting rule in Equation~\eqref{eq:p2weighted} with all cells and the same fixed 50-step prediction, equal cell weights gave a 49.40\% improvement and equal donor mass gave 49.69\%. The three donor-specific improvements under this pooled field were 47.15\%, 46.92\% and 40.07\% (Table~\ref{tab:p2weights}). This sensitivity changes the evaluation measure without refitting the field; it is distinct from the donor-transfer experiment and from the pooled capped-distance estimate.

The result is deliberately bounded. The donors are healthy volunteers, the wounds are acute experimental wounds and the target is one observed time point. The analysis does not contain a DFU patient trajectory or a time-to-closure endpoint. ``Transfer'' here means transfer across the three specified acute-wound donors under a held-out protocol; it does not mean clinical generalization.

Within-donor interpolation uses an observed later biopsy from the donor being reconstructed, which is permitted by the global timepoint-holdout task. It would not be available in prospective source-only prediction for a new donor. Conversely, the donor-transfer benchmark observes the target time in the training donors and asks a different question. Its method comparisons cannot be generalized to a globally unobserved time. The probability-conservation identity in Equation~\eqref{eq:p2continuity} concerns the constructed training paths, not identification of a biological trajectory. The observed reconstruction gains remain conditional on this representation, interval and three donors (Tables~\ref{tab:p2folds} and~\ref{tab:p2geometrytable}). Improvement over unchanged source alone does not establish incremental value over other permitted observations.

\subsection{Simple interpolation is competitive when an entire time point is missing}

A retrospective extension tested whether improving over unchanged source requires a neural field. The additional comparisons hold out either day 7 or day 1 globally, keeping the frozen encoder, rank clock, training-only scaling and optimization budget fixed. For each task, the observed time immediately before the target supplies the source and the next observed time supplies an anchor. The source and anchor belong to the same donor; the held-time cells enter only the evaluation. Error is calculated using every available cell in all 15 coordinates within each donor, then averaged with equal donor weight. The donor-averaged error in Equation~\eqref{eq:p2macroerror} differs from the earlier pooled and capped estimands. The two endpoint controls in Equation~\eqref{eq:p2interpolation} therefore address model complexity under this separate, explicit scoring rule.

For withheld day 7, unchanged source had mean donor error 1.4725. Across three seeds, shared flow reduced it to 0.8110, while within-donor centroid translation reduced it further to 0.6674. An independent-endpoint linear bridge gave 0.9284 (Figure~\ref{fig:p2f3}A\label{call:p2f3}). The corresponding improvements were 44.9\%, 54.7\% and 36.9\%. That bridge is the one-segment population target of the product-coupling objective. The shared field's lower error is compatible with multi-segment fitting and finite capacity; it does not show that the objective targets a different interpolant, and translation remained lower. Withholding day 1 instead trained on intact skin, day 7 and day 30 and predicted from intact skin. The unchanged-source error was 1.3460; shared flow, centroid translation and the independent bridge gave 1.0571, 0.9293 and 1.0959, corresponding to improvements of 21.5\%, 31.0\% and 18.6\% (Figure~\ref{fig:p2f3}B). The complete seed-level errors appear in Table~\ref{tab:p2interpolation}.

All 354 original fields and nine selected outer refits reproduced their full-source predictions exactly on CUDA; all 31 cached coordinate matrices also reproduced exactly from the input. A separate recomputation matched 2,700 deterministic-control evaluation rows to within $4.5\times10^{-16}$. These checks support the saved computations, with the retrospective training-provenance and alpha-grid limits stated in Methods. The earlier CPU-portability failure remains unresolved.

For missing day 7, OT-coupled log2 energy ratios to translation were $-0.026$, 0.321 and 0.351 in PWH26, PWH27 and PWH28; entropic-bridge ratios were 0.016, 0.284 and 0.192 (Figure~\ref{fig:p2f9}A\label{call:p2f9}; Table~\ref{tab:p2followcoupling}). Thus OT coupling favored flow in one donor, and neither coupled family favored flow in at least two donors. Withheld-day-1 OT ratios were $-0.145$, 0.183 and 0.122. Independent 40,000-step fits remained above translation in all three day-7 donors. These comparisons do not certify optimizer convergence or universal inferiority.

Across all five coordinate systems, the donor-mean day-7 ratio favored translation for both independent and OT fields (Figure~\ref{fig:p2f9}B; Table~\ref{tab:p2followrepresentation}). Individual donor signs nevertheless changed: the day-7 OT advantage in one donor under standardized coordinates disappeared under whitening and training PCA. Held-donor OT likewise favored one donor under standardized coordinates but none under centered raw or whitened coordinates. Repeated aggregate ordering does not make donor-specific conclusions invariant to the geometry.

Nested selection reduced held-donor day-1-to-day-7 errors compared with the default field, while leaving the comparison with the target marginal donor-specific (Figure~\ref{fig:p2f9}C; Table~\ref{tab:p2follownested}). Selected-field log2 ratios to the marginal were 1.991, 0.825 and $-0.471$, versus default values 2.372, 3.123 and $-0.419$. The bounded inner search improves this computational comparison without adding a donor or establishing population superiority.

The nested mixture in Equation~\eqref{eq:p2shrinkage} also retained contrary results (Figure~\ref{fig:p2f9}D; Table~\ref{tab:p2followshrinkage}). Day-1-to-day-7 independent-flow mixtures used weight 0.10 in every donor and gave log2 ratios of draw-averaged energy to the marginal of $-0.029$, 0.045 and $-0.143$. Selected mixtures therefore improved on the marginal in two donors and worsened it in one. These ratio-of-means values are a different estimand from the matched-draw means above. The geometry summaries alone do not determine whether source substitution helps or whether a held donor is outside the training population.

Training-cell decoding gave held-donor mean-expression total variation of 0.171 for independent flow, 0.149 for OT flow, 0.144 for location--scale and 0.113 for the target marginal (Figure~\ref{fig:p2f9}E; Table~\ref{tab:p2followdecode}). Decoding the observed target gave 0.084, showing substantial decoder mismatch even at observed coordinates. These errors assess mean gene proportions under an empirical decoder; they neither establish faithful cellwise expression nor replace the earlier topic-decoder audit. The displayed comparison contains all three held donors; the one-donor missing-time diagnostic is not promoted to a cohort result.

The target-informed alpha scan found a day-7 donor-mean minimum at alpha 0.55 for translation and 0.45 for OT flow (Figure~\ref{fig:p2f9}F; Table~\ref{tab:p2followalpha}). Their minimum errors were 0.700 and 0.817, compared with 0.705 and 0.819 at rank alpha 0.5. Changing the evaluation location of a fixed rank-trained field is distinct from the refitted-clock experiment. These oracle scans describe error surfaces after viewing the held target and do not select a validated temporal coordinate.
