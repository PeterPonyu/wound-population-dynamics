{\fontsize{10}{13}\selectfont Information available in the two temporal prediction tasks. Three healthy human donors contribute skin and wound specimens on days 0, 1, 7 and 30. (A) Global timepoint holdout excludes every day-7 cell from model fitting and scaling. Blue tiles identify training samples; teal outlines also identify the observed day-1 source of prediction; hatched orange tiles are evaluation targets. (B) One illustrative donor-transfer fold excludes all cells of the third donor from fitting and scaling. Only its day-1 cells initialize prediction, its day-7 cells are evaluated, and its grey day-0/day-30 samples are unused. All four times remain available in the other two donors, and the test-donor role is rotated across all three. (C) A frozen expression map and training-only scaling precede prediction and energy-distance comparison with an unchanged source distribution. Time coordinates, donor count, training seeds, integration resolution and evaluation sampling provide complementary sensitivity checks. The arrays describe samples, not tracked cells; neither task observes diabetic-foot-ulcer healing or time to closure.\par}

{\fontsize{10}{13}\selectfont Global Wound7 reconstruction, donor transfer and donor--time geometry. (A) Bars show predicted and stay-still energy distances to pooled Wound7 after excluding that time globally; relative improvement is 100 times (stay-still minus predicted distance) divided by stay-still distance, giving 49.9\%. (B) Each bar is a genuinely held-out donor, with all four times retained in the other two donors; the mean relative improvement is 75.9\%. Bars are point estimates without confidence intervals. (C) Points show 12 between-donor same-time distances and 18 within-donor between-time distances. Boxes span the interquartile range, centre lines are medians, and whiskers extend to extreme points within 1.5 interquartile ranges. The temporal/donor median ratio is 8.46 (10,000-draw descriptive pair-distance bootstrap 95\% interval 6.02--13.11). All distances use training-standardized latent coordinates. Shared donors make these pairs dependent; three healthy donors do not provide DFU prediction validation.\par}

{\fontsize{10}{13}\selectfont Time-coordinate sensitivity under global Wound7 holdout in three donors. {\fontsize{10}{13}\selectfont Method comparison across three held-out donors. (A) Bars are arithmetic mean energy distances for stay-still and five predictors evaluated on the same donor folds. CFM means conditional flow matching; OT is the entropic optimal-transport displacement extension. Shared CFM has the lowest point estimate (0.395). Each predictive method improves over stay-still by more than the single split-half target reference in 3/3 folds; this is a descriptive criterion, not a significance test or population method ranking. (B) Each line connects shared and donor-conditioned errors for one of six training-donor comparisons (two retained donors in each of three fits). Conditioning improves all six. These training-donor fits share donors and do not measure transfer to an unseen donor. Neither panel shows uncertainty intervals.\par}

{\fontsize{10}{13}\selectfont Donor-count comparison and training-seed variation. (A) k is the number of training donors. The line joins seed-0 mean held-out energy distances, 0.5496 at k=1 and 0.3854 at k=2; symbols identify seeds 0, 1 and 2. At k=1, the two single-training-donor choices are averaged within each held donor before equally weighting the three held donors. (B) Points are seed-specific reductions in mean energy distance (ED), k=1 minus k=2. The horizontal line is the three-seed mean, 0.165. Shading is a percentile 95\% interval [0.081, 0.315] from 4,000 resamples of three held-donor blocks after seed averaging; it is not an interval for each seed point. Evaluation subsampling is fixed across seeds. The largest configuration-specific range is 0.368. Overlapping training sets limit interval interpretation, and two k values cannot identify a plateau or required cohort size.\par}
